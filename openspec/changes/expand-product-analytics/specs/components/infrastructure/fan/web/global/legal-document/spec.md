## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Privacy Policy discloses session recordings

The Privacy Policy SHALL state that, while the Session replay setting is on, the fan's screens are recorded for replay with all text and input values masked, that the checkout, lottery entry, identity check and Settings screens are never recorded, that the recordings are transferred to PostHog (Klant Solutions B.V., Netherlands) with the usage analytics data, their purpose (improving the Service), and that the Session replay toggle in Settings stops recording.

#### Scenario: Recording disclosed
- **WHEN** the Privacy Policy is displayed
- **THEN** it lists masked session recordings among the data transferred to PostHog, names the screens never recorded, and says how to stop recording
