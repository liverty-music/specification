## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The Privacy Policy states what remains after withdrawal

The Privacy Policy SHALL state that a fan can delete their account from Settings at any time unless a ticket for an upcoming event, a listed resale, a lottery entry awaiting its draw or a purchase being paid is in progress; that deletion removes the account, its sign-in, follows, notification settings, passkeys and identity check (本人確認) result; that order and ticket records are kept after deletion for accounting, tax and dispute handling; and that usage analytics data already collected is kept in a form that no longer identifies the fan. The retention period SHALL be stated as a number of years. Whether the wording legally suffices is a matter for the policy owner / legal counsel to confirm and is out of scope for this spec.

#### Scenario: Withdrawal disclosed

- **WHEN** the Privacy Policy is displayed
- **THEN** it states how to delete an account, what blocks it, what is removed, that order and ticket records are kept with their retention period in years and why, and what happens to analytics data
