## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Terms of Service state the official resale rules

The Terms of Service SHALL include an official resale (公式リセール) article stating:
- that resale through the platform at face value is the only resale the Organizer consents to;
- that resale opens for a seat type of an event only when that seat type sells out, and that a fan who cannot attend an event whose seat type does not sell out has no resale and no refund except when the event is 中止 (cancelled);
- that a resale ticket is not bought first come but allocated by a draw every day at 12:00 Japan time and, on the event's day, at 12:00 or 3 hours before the start time, whichever is earlier, which is the last draw and the resale deadline; that a group's tickets are allocated all together or not at all; and that the total is held on the fan's card from the application until the entry wins, lapses, loses or is withdrawn, is charged only when the fan wins, and is given back otherwise;
- that an application can be withdrawn while it waits for a draw, that an application whose card authorization would expire before it could be charged ends and can be made again, and that a win whose card cannot be charged is cancelled;
- that listing closes at the resale deadline, that an unsold ticket stays the fan's and remains valid, and that a listed ticket can be withdrawn until a draw allocates it, and that a sale is not guaranteed;
- that when the ticket is allocated and the winning fan is charged, it becomes invalid, a new ticket is issued to the winning fan, and the listing fan is refunded the face value minus a resale fee of 10% to the card they paid with;
- that when a concert is cancelled after a resale, the resold ticket's fee is not returned.

#### Scenario: Resale article present
- **WHEN** the Terms of Service is displayed
- **THEN** it states when resale opens, that tickets are allocated by a daily draw and charged only on a win, the resale deadline, that a seat type that does not sell out has no resale, the 10% resale fee, and how the refund is made
